## Featured: AI Travel Video Editor

**Your travel footage. Your memories. A film with a story.**

[Family Travel Editor](https://github.com/denyakortunov/family-travel-editor) is a free, open-source skill for an AI agent. It grew out of editing my own family trips: camera and drone footage, photo GPS, travel records, questions that recover the story, my recorded voiceover, and comments on each edit.

The workflow is editor-independent. I use DaVinci Resolve; you can use the plans with your preferred editor. The repository includes Python helpers, a silent FFmpeg preview and a reproducible trimming test. Full editing still needs the right tools and your creative decisions.
